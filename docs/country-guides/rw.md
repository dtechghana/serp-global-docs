## HR & Statutory Deductions

| Deduction | Rate | Body |
|-----------|------|------|
| PAYE | Progressive bands | RRA |
| RSSB (Pension — employee) | 5% | RSSB |
| RSSB (Pension — employer) | 5% | RSSB |
| RSSB (Medical — employee) | 7.5% | RSSB |
| RSSB (Medical — employer) | 7.5% | RSSB |
| RSSB (Maternity — employer) | 0.3% | RSSB |

!!! warning
    Rwandan statutory rates are governed by the RSSB Act and annual Finance Laws. The RSSB manages both the Caisse Sociale du Rwanda (CSF) and health insurance contributions. Verify current rates before running payroll.

## Notes

- Rwanda operates on GMT+2 (Central Africa Time) year-round
- The academic year runs from January to November (3 terms)
- English became the primary language of instruction in 2009; French and Kinyarwanda are also official languages
- MTN Mobile Money is the dominant mobile payment platform
