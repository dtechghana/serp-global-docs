## Academic Structure

Kenya's Ministry of Education CBC (Competency Based Curriculum) system:

| sERP Division | KE Level | Classes |
|---------------|----------|---------|
| Pre-Primary | PP | PP1–PP2 |
| Lower Primary | Lower Primary | Grade 1–3 |
| Upper Primary | Upper Primary | Grade 4–6 |
| Junior Secondary | JSS | Grade 7–9 |
| Senior Secondary | Senior School | Grade 10–12 |

!!! note
    Kenya is transitioning from the 8-4-4 system to CBC. Schools completing the transition may still reference Standard 7–8 / Form 1–4 class labels.

## HR & Statutory Deductions

| Deduction | Rate | Body |
|-----------|------|------|
| PAYE (Pay As You Earn) | Progressive bands | KRA |
| NSSF (employee) | 6% (Tier I + II) | NSSF |
| NSSF (employer) | 6% (Tier I + II) | NSSF |
| SHA / NHIF (employee) | 2.75% of gross | SHA |
| Housing Levy (employee) | 1.5% | NSSF / NHDF |
| Housing Levy (employer) | 1.5% | NSSF / NHDF |

!!! warning
    Kenya's NSSF contribution rates were revised under the NSSF Act 2013 and are subject to ongoing legal interpretation. The Affordable Housing Levy and SHA (formerly NHIF) rates have also changed recently. Verify current rates before running payroll.

## Notes

- Kenya operates on GMT+3 (East Africa Time) year-round
- The academic year runs January to November (3 terms)
- English is the language of instruction; Swahili is a co-official language
- M-Pesa (Safaricom) is the dominant mobile money platform
