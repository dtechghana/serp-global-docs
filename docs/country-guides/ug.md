!!! warning
    Ugandan statutory rates are governed by the Income Tax Act and the NSSF Act. Verify current rates before running payroll.

## Notes

- Uganda operates on GMT+3 (East Africa Time) year-round
- The academic year typically runs February to December (3 terms)
- English is the official language of instruction; Luganda and other local languages are widely spoken
- MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money are the dominant mobile money platforms
