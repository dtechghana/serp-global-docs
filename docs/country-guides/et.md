## Notes

- Ethiopia operates on GMT+3 (East Africa Time) year-round
- The academic year runs September to July (2 semesters), aligned to the Ethiopian calendar year
- Ethiopia uses its own Ge'ez calendar (13 months); sERP uses the Gregorian calendar for all dates
- Amharic is the working language of the federal government; English is the medium of instruction from Grade 5
- Telebirr (Ethio Telecom) and CBE Birr (Commercial Bank of Ethiopia) are the primary mobile money platforms
