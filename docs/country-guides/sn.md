## Notes

- XOF is shared with Côte d'Ivoire and other UEMOA countries; the exchange rate to EUR is fixed
- The academic year typically runs October to July
- Senegal operates on GMT+0 year-round (no daylight saving)
- French is the primary language of instruction; Wolof and other national languages are widely spoken
