## HR & Statutory Deductions

| Deduction | Rate | Body |
|-----------|------|------|
| PAYE (Pay As You Earn) | Progressive bands | TRA |
| NSSF (employee) | 10% | NSSF |
| NSSF (employer) | 10% | NSSF |
| SDL (Skills Development Levy, employer) | 4% of gross payroll | TRA |
| WHT (Workers Compensation Fund, employer) | Varies by sector | WCF |

!!! warning
    Tanzanian statutory rates are governed by the Income Tax Act and the NSSF Act. The SDL is also employer-mandatory. Verify current rates before running payroll.

## Notes

- Tanzania operates on GMT+3 (East Africa Time) year-round
- The academic year runs from January to November (3 terms)
- Swahili (Kiswahili) is the language of instruction in primary; English in secondary
- M-Pesa, Airtel Money and Tigo Pesa (Miitel) are all widely used for school fee collection
